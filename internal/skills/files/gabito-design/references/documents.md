# Documents (Word, PDF, reports, letters)

Use together with the docx or pdf skill that writes the file. Design lives in
styles, not in manual formatting.

## Principles

- **Styles, not hand formatting.** Define Title, Heading 1 to 3, Body, Caption,
  Quote, List, Table styles once; apply them everywhere. Never fake a heading with
  bold text, or spacing with empty paragraphs or tabs.
- **Page first.** Set page size (A4 or Letter by audience), margins (2 to 2.5 cm
  or 0.8 to 1 in), and a baseline rhythm before touching content.
- **Readable measure.** Body in an 10 to 12 pt face, line spacing 1.15 to 1.5,
  50 to 80 characters per line. Wide pages get wider margins or two columns, not
  longer lines.
- **Outline as navigation.** Headings should read as a table of contents on
  their own. Add a generated contents list for documents over about six pages.
- **Hierarchy with restraint.** Three heading levels, one accent color, one or
  two typefaces. Emphasis by weight; underline is for links only.
- **Orientation.** Title, subtitle, author, date, version; page numbers; running
  header or footer with the short title; consistent first-page treatment.
- **Tables are for comparison.** Header row distinct, light horizontal rules, no
  heavy grid, right-aligned numbers, units in headers, repeat header rows across
  pages.
- **Figures.** Numbered, captioned, referenced in the text, near their mention,
  sized to the text width, with alt text. Do not let figures split from captions.

## Quality floor

- Real styles used throughout; the navigation pane shows a sensible outline.
- No orphan headings at a page bottom; no single lines stranded on a page; no
  table row split across pages without need (keep rows together).
- Fonts embedded or common; fallbacks acceptable in both Word and PDF.
- Contrast and print: text at least 4.5:1 on its background and legible in
  grayscale; no color-only meaning.
- Hyphenation and justification only if the result has no rivers; otherwise left-aligned.
- Accessible output: document title and language set, tagged PDF with reading
  order, alt text, real list and table structures, meaningful link text.
- Quotes, dashes and non-breaking spaces typeset properly (curly quotes, en dash
  for ranges, a non-breaking space between a number and its unit).
- Metadata clean: no stale author, comments or tracked changes in a final file.

## Layouts by kind

- **Report:** cover or title block, summary of one page with the decision and the
  numbers, contents, numbered sections, appendix. Summary first, method later.
- **Letter or memo:** sender, date, recipient, subject line stating the point,
  short paragraphs, clear ask in the first three lines, signature.
- **One-pager:** one message, three supporting blocks, one number that matters,
  a contact or next step. If you need a smaller font to fit, cut content.
- **Proposal or contract:** numbered clauses with consistent cross-references,
  defined terms in bold at first use, signature block that stays on one page.
- **Resume or CV:** one column or a narrow sidebar, reverse chronology, strong
  verbs, consistent dates, no photos or ratings bars unless required.

## Document anti-patterns

- A wall of text with no headings, or headings on every paragraph.
- Title in a decorative font and body in a different decorative font.
- Heavy colored banners behind every heading; clip art; WordArt effects.
- Tables with full grids and shaded alternating rows in saturated color.
- Bullets for everything, including sentences that should be a paragraph; bullets
  nested more than two levels.
- Manual line breaks to push text to the next page; spaces to align columns.
- Bold, italic, underline and color combined on one phrase.
- Full-justified narrow columns with large gaps.
- Cover pages with a stock photo and a gradient overlay on a report that needs none.
- Auto-generated filler: generic introductions that restate the title, summaries
  that repeat the previous paragraph, "In conclusion" paragraphs that add nothing.
- Screenshots pasted at low resolution; charts pasted as blurry images.
- A table of contents that is out of date, or page numbers that restart by accident.

## Check before handing over

Open in the target app and in PDF; page through every page; look at the outline;
update fields; check one page in grayscale; confirm the file name, title and
language. State what you could not check (fonts on the reader's machine).
