# Modes

A mode is a stance: what the design optimizes for. Choose by the surface, then
let the mode set density, tone, pacing and the acceptable level of risk.

| | Persuade | Experience | Operate | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | move to one action | immerse and guide | get work done | understand and retain |
| Density | low | low to medium | medium to high | medium |
| Contrast of scale | high | high, with pacing | restrained | restrained |
| Motion | purposeful, short | central, orchestrated | minimal, functional | almost none |
| Type | expressive display + calm body | expressive, varied | neutral, compact, tabular | comfortable serif or sans |
| Color | a strong accent and bold moments | mood-driven palette | neutral base, semantic only | near-monochrome |
| Risk | can be bold | can be distinctive | must be predictable | must be quiet |

## Persuade

For marketing and landing pages, pitch decks, posters, ads, launch announcements.

- One message and one next step. If there are two, one is secondary and looks it.
- Open with the benefit in the reader's words; proof follows (numbers, names, quotes).
- Use scale contrast: a very large headline against calm supporting text.
- Sections breathe and vary in rhythm; do not stack identical blocks.
- Use a specific image or product view, not a generic stock scene.
- The call to action repeats at natural decision points with the same wording.
- Trust signals sit near the decision: pricing, guarantees, logos, real testimonials.
- Check: can a stranger say what it is, who it is for and what to do in five seconds?

## Experience

For product onboarding, immersive sites, portfolios, editorial features, apps where
feeling matters as much as function.

- Design the sequence: arrival, orientation, discovery, payoff, exit.
- Pace the reveal; give a pause between intense moments.
- Motion carries meaning: continuity between screens, cause and effect.
- Let the content set the mood; the palette and type follow the subject.
- Keep a reliable way out: navigation, skip, reduced-motion path, keyboard path.
- Performance is part of the experience: first paint fast, no jank.
- Check: remove the animation; does it still make sense and feel considered?

## Operate

For dashboards, internal tools, admin panels, forms, data tables, spreadsheets.

- Speed beats charm. Prefer familiar patterns; novelty costs the user time.
- Density is a feature, but each row and cell remains readable: align numbers
  right with tabular figures, left-align text, keep row height consistent.
- Show state clearly: selected, changed, saving, saved, failed.
- Forms: label above the field, visible labels, errors next to the field and in
  a summary, never clear input on error, sensible defaults, keyboard order.
- Make the frequent action one step; keep destructive actions apart and confirmable.
- Color is for meaning (status, alerts), not decoration.
- Check: can a regular user complete the top task without the mouse, twice as fast
  as before?

## Read

For long documents, reports, articles, docs, ebooks, READMEs, contracts.

- The page serves the text. Margins and measure first, ornament never.
- Body 10 to 12 pt in print, 16 to 20 px on screen, line height around 1.5,
  line length 45 to 75 characters.
- Headings form a scannable outline; the first line under each states the point.
- Use lists for parallel items, tables for comparison, callouts sparingly (one
  or two per page at most).
- Keep figures near the text that cites them, each with a caption and a number.
- Provide orientation: title, date, version, contents, page numbers, running heads.
- Check: can someone skim the headings and bold lead-ins and get the argument?

## Mixing and switching

- A landing page that contains a pricing table: Persuade overall, Operate inside the table.
- A report with an executive summary slide: Read for the body, Persuade for the summary.
- A dashboard's empty state: Operate overall, a small Experience touch for the first run.
- If the user states a feel ("playful", "serious", "dense"), keep the mode and
  tune tone and density inside its limits. If they contradict the mode, say so
  and offer the trade-off.
